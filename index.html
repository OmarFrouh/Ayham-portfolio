Method

A practice of listening
before drawing.

i

Listen

Long conversations. Site visits without a sketchbook. Understanding the room behind the room.

ii

Frame

Establishing the brief as a question of feeling, not just function. The mood, the weight, the air.

iii

Draw

Plans, sections, prototypes. The slow conversation between hand, page, and material sample.

iv

Build

On site with the trades. Adjusting, refining. The last 5% is where the work earns its keep.
